# Hafiz Trademark Policy

"Hafiz" and the Hafiz logo (the "Marks") are trademarks of Irshad Ali. This
policy explains how you may and may not use them.

The goal is simple: keep the name useful to the community while preventing
confusion about who made Hafiz and who stands behind a given build.

## You may, without asking

- **Refer to Hafiz by name** in articles, tutorials, blog posts, conference
  talks, social media, and documentation.
- **Say your software works with Hafiz** — phrases like "works with Hafiz,"
  "compatible with Hafiz," "built on Hafiz," or "integrates with Hafiz" are
  explicitly permitted (this is called *nominative fair use*).
- **Use the name in factual package metadata**, e.g. `hafiz-plugin-foo` on
  PyPI, provided your package genuinely extends Hafiz and does not imply
  official endorsement.
- **Distribute unmodified copies** of Hafiz under its license, keeping the
  name and attribution intact.

## You may not, without written permission

- **Name a fork, derivative, or competing product "Hafiz"** or any
  confusingly similar name (e.g. "Hafiz Cloud," "Hafiz Pro," "Hafyz,"
  "Hafiz++").
- **Use the Marks in a way that implies official endorsement, affiliation, or
  partnership** with the Hafiz project or Irshad Ali when none exists.
- **Register the Marks** (or confusingly similar marks) as trademarks, domain
  names, social media handles, or company names in any jurisdiction.
- **Use the Hafiz logo** in modified form, or apply it to products or services
  other than Hafiz itself.

## Forks and derivatives

You are free to fork Hafiz under the terms of the [LICENSE](LICENSE) — but
forks must ship under a different name. A descriptive prefix or suffix that
makes the distinction clear (e.g. "MyOrg Memory Layer, forked from Hafiz") is
fine; reusing "Hafiz" as the primary identifier is not.

## Commercial licensing

If you want to use the Marks in a way this policy does not permit — a
managed service, a branded appliance, a commercial distribution — contact
the maintainer to discuss a trademark license. Trademark permission is
separate from the code license.
